<ul class="U01_bullet">
<li class="U01_bull10">Brainstorm related words. Say, <span class="U01_blue">&#x201C;To help answer the essential question, I first want to think of words that might describe someone who responds to a challenge. <li class="U01_bull10">Ask students to share other words that describe someone who responds to a challenge. Add new words to column 1 as a class.</li>
</ul>
<p class="U01_noindent1"><span class="U06_toccurb1">APPLY</span></p>
<ul class="U01_bullet">
<li class="U01_bull10">Have students work in small groups to brainstorm words related to overcome and challenge.</li>
<li class="U01_bull10">Use a Response Protocol to have students share their words. Clarify any misunderstandings if words are not related to or synonyms for overcome and challenge.</li>
</ul>
